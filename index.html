Entrepreneurship + Entry Role Snapshot

Make the path concrete: management and entrepreneurship students can test broad interests through specific entry points such as leadership development program associate, operations analyst, associate program manager, founder-in-residence, startup operations associate, and HR business partner associate. Kogod's Veloric Center for Entrepreneurship offers a practical support ecosystem including entrepreneurship coursework, competitions and events, the AU Business Incubator, Eagle Venture Seed Fund, Eagle Angel Network, and cross-campus partnerships.
